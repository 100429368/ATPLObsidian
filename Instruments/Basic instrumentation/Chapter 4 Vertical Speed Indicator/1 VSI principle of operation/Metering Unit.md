The VSI metering unit is a chokethat produces a tie delay of about for seconds between the ambient and recent (past) static pressure. 

The tie delay must remain constant despite changes in the air's pressure,temperature and viscosity. The metering unit copes with these changes by diving the flow into turbulent (induces by an origice or knife edge) and laminar ( through a capillary ).

The two flows are affected differently by the air's properties. This keeps the overall flow rate aproximately constant.
