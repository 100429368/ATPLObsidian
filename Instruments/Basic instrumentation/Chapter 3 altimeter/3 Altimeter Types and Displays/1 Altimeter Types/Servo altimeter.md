**Servo Altimeter**: Is found on smaller to medium sized aeroplanes which do not have air data computers. More accurate than the sensitive altimeter. Lag is virtually eliminatedbecause there is no mechanical gear involved. 

Uses anelectromagnetic E and I bar transducer system. Movement ofthe aneroid capsules causes the I bar to rotate out of alignment with the E bar.

The un equal air gaps between the E and I bars result in an induced current in the E bar. This activates a servo motor which drives the E bar back into alignment with the I bar - the null position.

In doing on the servo motor also drives the altimeterpoints to their new positions

Servo altimeter is more sensitive and accurate than the sensitive altimeter because the capsule stackonly has toovercomethe hysteresis of the capsule. This type of altimeter may be accurate enough for use in airspace using reduced vertical sepration minima (RVSM), because it is accurate and has altitude reporting. However,it too is now almost obsolete in CAT operations.
