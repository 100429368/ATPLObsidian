It is based on the measurement of atmospheric static pressure, which reduces with an increase in vertical distance.

A barometric or pressure altimeter is a pressure instrument, which senses static pressure at its location. 

Static pressure is predictable with vertical distance,therefore the altimeter indicates the difference between the two pressures asvertical distance above the reference pressure. 

The lower the altitude, the greater the static pressure and the smallerthe difference frothe reference pressure.

The reference pressure is not a constant value, and must be entered in the sub-scale of the altimeter by the pilot before use.


Mind that the  pressure and altitude, are related in a NON LINEAR WAY,if we are above 5000ft. Below 5000ft it can be approximated as lineal.
