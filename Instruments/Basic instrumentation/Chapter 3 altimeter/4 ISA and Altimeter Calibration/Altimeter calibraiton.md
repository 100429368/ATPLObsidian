The barometric altimeter is calibrated to display a change of vertical distance from a baseline elevation, at which a reference pressure setting has been determined, assuming a linear change of 30ft change of vertical distance per 1hPa change of pressure.

30ft/hPa is the quoted value in the learning objectives, and is approximately equivalent to the average rate in the ISA model, between sea level and 5000ft.

The actual rate of change of pressure in the ISA modelis non-linear. Above 5000ft the average rate changes significantly. At 20 000ft a rateof 51ft/hPa is shown and at 36 000 ft this increases to 100fh/hPa
