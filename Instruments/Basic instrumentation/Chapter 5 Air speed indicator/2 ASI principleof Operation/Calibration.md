The ASI shows indicated air speed (IAS). Indicated airspeed is rarely the same as the true airspeed of the aircraft. This is because dynamic pressure depends on the density of the air flowing,and the density of the air reduces with altitude.

For example,if an aircraft climbs at constant speed over the ground the indicated airspeed will slowly reduce.

Consecuently an ASI instrument is calibrated so that IAS and TAS are the same when the air density is 1.225kg/m^3 ,which is the air density at mean sea level according to ISA.
