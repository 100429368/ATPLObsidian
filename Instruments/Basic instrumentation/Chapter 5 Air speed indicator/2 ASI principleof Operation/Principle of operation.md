In a simple airspeed inciator (ASI) pitot pressure is fed directly into pressure capsule. Aspitot pressure indicatesthe capsule expands.

To substractthe contribution of the static pressure, a static port is connected directly to the sealed instrument case. Because static pressure is sensed inside and outside the capsule, any expansion or contraction of the capsule mustbe due solely to the dynamic pressure.

The movement of the capsule is magnified and transmitted to the ASI pointer via temperature. And scale-correcting linkage.
