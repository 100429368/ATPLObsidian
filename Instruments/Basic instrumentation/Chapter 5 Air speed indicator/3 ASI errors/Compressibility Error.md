The same ASI is calibrated on the assumption that airflow is not compressible. This is true at lower airspeeds,and up to about 300ft TAS,compressibility errors are negligible.

At higher speeds,air entering the capsule via the pitot becomes compressed,causing the ASI to overread. The actual amount of compressibility error depends on the IAS and ambient conditions. 


Above 300kt TAS,the effects of compressibility become noticeable. On aircraft without sophisticated ADCs you may need to use a navication computer to calculate the required correction for compressibility error .
