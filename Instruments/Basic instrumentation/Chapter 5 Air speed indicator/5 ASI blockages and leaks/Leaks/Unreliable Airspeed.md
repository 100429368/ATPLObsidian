On commercial aircraftthere are usually three ASIs -one for each pilot ad a standby instrumet. There are two ormorepitot systems,and to or more static systems, with each pilot's ASI connected to different systems.

This provides a substantial level of redundancy but can cause difficulty in identifying which ASI is correct when thereis a disagreement between them. At first glance you can not tell wether your ASI is overreading,or the other ASI is undereading.


ERROR
Failing to deal efectively with unreliable airspeed indicators quickly leading to loss of situation awareness.
In such circumstances you must use help to identify the faulty dispplay:
* Airframe buffeting:indicating either high Mach number or low airspeed.
* Unexpected aircraft behaviour: Crisp responses to flight control inputs implying high speed;slugish responses implying lowspeed.
* System warnings: Most aircraft. Have warnings systems alerting crew to even very small airspeed discrepancies.
* Unusual combinations of altitude and power.
