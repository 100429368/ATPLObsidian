Total(or pitot) pressure= dynamic pressure + static pressure
![[Pasted image 20261105183423.png]]

To measure just the dynamic pressure we must find a practical way to subsctact the contribution of staticpressure from pitot pressure. The simplest way is to fix a flexibl strip at one end and expose it to the air flowing past the aircraft. The faster the airflow,the more the strip bends.

Pitot pressure acts only on the side of the flexible strip. Static pressure operates on both sides. The practical effect is that the static pressure is cancelled out,the only thing causing the indicator to bend its dynamic pressure.

We compare the bending to know thespeed. The speed which is indicated by the device is called the indicated airspeed (IAS)
