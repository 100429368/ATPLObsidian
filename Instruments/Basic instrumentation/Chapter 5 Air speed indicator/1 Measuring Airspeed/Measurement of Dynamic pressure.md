Airspeed

True Air Speed TAS is the actualspeed of the aeroplane through the air. TAS is useful for flight planning and navigation.

To control the aircraft wichin a safe margin of its capability, the pilot is most interested in a value of speed which directly relatesto aerodynamic performance.

Aerodynamic performance depends mostly on the massflow rate of the air around the wings $\frac{1}{2}\rho v^2$
Dynamic pressure is what we need to measure,to display a speed which is relevant to aerodynamicperformance and capability.
