Equivalent Airspeed is corrected for compressibility error. EAS provides the most accurate indication of dynamic pressure.

At slower speed IAS,CAS and EASareeither the same or differ by only a few knots. At high speed the difference between CAS and EAS becomes noticeable.

EAS is rarely used because, at high altitude or high speed,mach number is the preffered measure of speed.

$V_{MO}$,the maximum operating airspeed, is the onlyoperating speed based on EAS,all others are based,either on IAS,or on CAS if the aircraft has an air data computer.
