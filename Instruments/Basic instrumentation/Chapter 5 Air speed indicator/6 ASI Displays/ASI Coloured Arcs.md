
Conventional ASIS in light aircraft have three coloured arcs. The white arc shows the flap operating range. The lower end of the white arc is $V_{so}$ ,the stall speedat the maximum certified take-of mass(MCTOM) is the landing configuration (fullflaps,gear down,power off). The high end of the white arc is the fastest speed allowed with flaps extended ($V_{FE}$)

The upper limit of the green arc is $V_{NO}$, which is the maximum operating airspeed.
