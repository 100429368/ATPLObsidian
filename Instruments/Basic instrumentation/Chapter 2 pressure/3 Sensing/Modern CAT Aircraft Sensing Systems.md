CAT have numerous systems requiring static and pitot pressure.

Sensors are MEMS, based in piezoresistors. The resistance changes when pressure change. This is all sensed by a smallcomputer,called ADM(air data module). This ADM converts resistance to a digital value. 


Modern aircraft use multi-function probes,they measure:
* AoA (Angle of attack)
* TAT (totalair temperature)
* Total pressure

They use MEMS sensors that are extremely accurate. 
Data processing is built in.

The most modern CAT aircraft use side-slip indicators(SSIs)(Boeing) /Sideslip angle sensors (SSA)(Airbus) that measure the non-axial airflow angle caused by aircraft manoeuvre and asymmetric flight.
