CAT has a ptt switch. The transmitter is deactivated when the switch isreleased once it springs back.

The switch can be stuck, 'Suck on transmit'. All background noise and voice conversations are broadcasted in the meantime. This can blockout almost all communication between other aircraft and ATC.

The effective 'jamming'of the frequency makes it impossiblefor any aircraft on the same frequeny to receive clearances in time,and for ATC to rescind clearances.
