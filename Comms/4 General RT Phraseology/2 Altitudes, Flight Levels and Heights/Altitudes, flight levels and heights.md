The reference pressure QNH is 1013.2hPa,or a QFRE


1. Altitude. Is vertical distance to aicraft fromsea level expressed in feet. The altimeterindicates altitude when a QNH is set. 
2. Flight level. The vertical distance of the airctaft above the standard pressure settings (SPS) datumof 1013.2hPais expressed as flight level. SPS is used at higher altitudes.
3. Height. Vertical distance of the aircraft above a fixed point expressed in feet. Thealtimeter reads the height above the aerodrome when aerodrome's QFE is set. QFE is not used atmost commercial aroports,but in smaller aerodromes and military.
