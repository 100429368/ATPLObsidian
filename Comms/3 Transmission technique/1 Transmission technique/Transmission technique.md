* Think about what you need to say and how you are going to say BEFORE pressing the transmit button
* Use normal conversational tone,speakclearly and distinctly at constant volume
* Mantain an even rate of 100 words per min
* If the recipient of your transmission needs to write down your message,speak at a slightly slower rate.
* A slight pause before and after numbers makes them easier to understand.
* Avoid using hesitation sounds 'um' 'er'
* Avoid excessive use of courtesies
* Use standard RT phraseology
