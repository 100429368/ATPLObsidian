Transmitters are tested, tests should be brief, ( less than 10 ).

Test message comprises:
* The callsign of the station being called
* The aircraft or ground station identifications
* The words 'radio check'
* The radio frequency being used
Readability:
* 1-UNREADABLE
* 2-READABLENOW AND THEN
* 3-READABLE BUT WITH DIFFICULTY
* 4-READABLE
* 5-PERFECTLY READABLE

Sometimes 5 in "lound and clear"
