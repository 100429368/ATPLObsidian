
The mayor of the RT comms occur in VHF.

VHF range is 108 MHz to 137 MHz.

* Lower frequency range (108 - 117.975MHz) for radio navigation systems
* Higher frequency range is used for the Aeronautical Mobile (route) Service,separated in:
	* Air-ground voice
	* Air-ground datalink
	* Air-air datalink

The highest frequencies, e.g. 136.975 MHz, are reserved for VHF datalink communications
