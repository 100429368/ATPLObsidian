* Air-ground Communication: Two way communication between aircraft and stations or locations on the surface of the Earth
* Air-to-ground Communication: One way communication from aircraft to stations or locations on the surface of the Earth
* Blind Transmission: Transmission from station to tation in circumstances where two way communication cannot be established but its believed that the station is able to receive.
* Broadcast: Transmission of information relating to air navigation that is not addressed to a specific station or station.
* Duplex: A method between two stations in both directions simultaneously.
* Ground-to-air Communication: One way communication from stations or locations in surface to aircraft.
* Interpilot-Air-to-Air Communication: Two way communication on the designated air-to-air channel to enable aircraft flying over remote and oceanic areas out of ranfe of VHF ground stations to exchange necessary operational information and to facilitate the resolution of operational problems.
* Non-network Communications: Radiotelephony communications conducted by a station of the aeronautical mobile service,other than those conducted as part of a radiotelephony network.
* Radiotelephony Network. Groupof aeronautical stations wich operate frequencies to ensure maximum dependability of air-ground ommunications and dissemination of air-ground traffic
* Readback. Procedure whereby the receiven station repeats a received message or an appropiate part thereof  back to the transmitting station so asto obtain confirmation of correct reception
* Simplex. One direction out of time communication
* Teleommunication. Any transmissionusing optical or electromagnetic systems.
