1. Aircraft registration (p,e SKYHAWK ECLPR or ECLPR)

3. The aircraftoperator's telephony designated followed by the flight identification

Full callsigns mustbe used when estabilishing communication

You may only abbreviate your callsign if it has first been abbreviated by the ground station.
