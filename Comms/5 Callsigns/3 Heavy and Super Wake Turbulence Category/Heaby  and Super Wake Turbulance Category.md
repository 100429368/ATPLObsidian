Behind every aircraft is a wake of turbulet air. Large, heavy aircraft create more turbulence.
Encountering this can be dangerous specially during departing or arriving. 
An aircraft in the 'heay' wake turbulence category ($\gt$ or equal to 136k) must use the suffix "heavy". A3080 uses 'Super'


MTOW = Maximum take off weight

Turbulent air according to kind of weight
1. Light - up to 7000km included - **L
2. Medium - 7000 kg to 136 000kg - **M 
3. Heayvy - 136 000 kg or more - **H
4. Super - Airbus388, A124, C5M - **J** (jumbo)
