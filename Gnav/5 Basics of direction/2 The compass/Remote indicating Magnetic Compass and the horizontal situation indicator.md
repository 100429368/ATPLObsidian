The DI is often replaced with a Horizontal Situation Indicator (HSI)

The HSI is a combination of DI with a CourseDeviation Indicator (CDI), used by reference to radio navigation aids.

HSI is connected to a remote indicating magnetic compass (RIMC), also knowin as fluxgate compass, which is a gyrocompass system that uses a remote sensor, located in the wingtip of tail of the aircraft.

The sensor, correctly known as magnemeter, detects the Earth's loal geomagnetic field, at the aircraft. 

Prior to flight HSI is synchronised, or slaved,with the sensor, and this keeps it aligned with the magnetic field and the direction of Magnetic North at the aircraft's location. 
The sensor is also prone to interference from other sources of magnetism, like those created by the engines and electrical systems. By locating it at one of the aircraft's extremities, such errors are minimised.
