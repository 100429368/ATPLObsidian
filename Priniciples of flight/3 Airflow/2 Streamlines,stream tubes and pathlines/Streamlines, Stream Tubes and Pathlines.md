We need: Direction of the flow and its speed

We can show the direction of a steady flow using lines which describe the direction of a very small quantity of flowing air. These are known as streamlines. At every point along the streamline,the line always and only shows the direction of the air stream at that exact point. 
A streamline can NEVER cros another streamline,because that would require two different speeds in the same point.

**A volume of air bounded by streamlines is known as a stream tube**
![[Pasted image 20261103203930.png]]

Since each of the streamlines comprising the boundary of the stream tube represents flow in one specific direction,the mass of air wichin the stream tube is only flowing along the stream tube and never inorput of it.

Wean say that,in a steady flow,the air inside a tube behaves as if it were inside a pipe with solid walls. Whatevermass of air enters the pipe,the same mass of air must exit it.
