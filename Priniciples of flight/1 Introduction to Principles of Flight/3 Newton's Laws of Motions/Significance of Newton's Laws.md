Because an airborne aircraft is in contact with nothing but air, the only way ir can oppose the force of gravity is to produce an opposing force by doing work on the air.

**An aircraft can only becaome airborne and stay airborne if it does work on the air to produce a force to oppose gravity.**

An aeroplane must produce a force that is exactly equal and opposite to the force of gravity if it is to remain airborne. That's the purpose of the wings. An aeroplane must produce thrust force which drives the wings through the air. This is the purpouse of the propeller or jet.

When an object moves through air,ir encounters resistance,caused by the air itself,called *drag*
