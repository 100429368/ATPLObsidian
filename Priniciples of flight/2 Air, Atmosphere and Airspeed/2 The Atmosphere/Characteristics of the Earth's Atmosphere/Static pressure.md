Particles in a fluid are constantly moving. When they collide with a surface they exert a force on which is felt as static pressure. This pressure is felt equally throughout the fluid and exerts an equal force in all directions.

**Air is fluid and thus static pressure is feltequally in al directions**

Static pressure is a force acting over an area and is measured in $N/m^2$

Air molecules are influenced by gravity and thus exert a downwards weight force. Because of this, the statcis pressure of the air increases as you desdend in the atmosphere:

**Static pressure reduces as altitude increases**

We have the same internal static pressure as the atmosphere, so we dont feel the diferences.
