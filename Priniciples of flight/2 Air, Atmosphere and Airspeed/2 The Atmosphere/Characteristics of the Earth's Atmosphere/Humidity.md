The atmosphere always contains some water vapour.

Completely dry air consists of:
 21%oxygen - atomic weigh of 16 ( 32 because $O_2$)
 78% nitrogen - atomic weight of 14 (28 because $N_2$) 

Consequently, the smallest unit of air has a molecular wight of 28.

Then $H_2 O$ has a molecular weight of 18, less dense. water vapour is much less massive than dry air.

**Air density reduces with increasing humidity**
