Air is viscous. Viscosity is the stickiness of a fluid. 
Describes the difficulty that molecules have when they try to flow past one another.

Viscosity also causes moving molecules to pull along (entrain) those adjacement to them. 

Viscosity measures the resistance that the molecules of a fluid to the movement. Viscosity will cause it to resist the objet's movement. The more disturbance created,the more resistant the fluid will be to an object moving through it.
