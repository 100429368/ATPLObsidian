It is a mesure of the change in the density of a certain fluid :
- Compressible fluid:its density can be changed (air)
- Incompressile fluid: its density remains constant (water,a liquid)

Sometimes a compressible fluid, can be taken as incompressible in determinated circunstances.

Mach numberdetermines the level ofhange of the density when the air is moving. If the mach number increases,the more compressible is the fluid.
