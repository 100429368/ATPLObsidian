$Dynamic Pressure=\frac{1}{2} \rho V^2= q$

$\rho$= density [kg/m^3]
V = TAS of the airflow 


**The amount of dynamic pressure depends on the airflow's density and its speed relative to the aircraft (TAS)**


A fluid will move from high pressure to low pressure. This creates an airflow.
