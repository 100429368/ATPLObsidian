The sensing device is expossed on one side total pressure and on the other, to static pressure only.

A small sampleof the airflow is introduced into a closed flexible capsule via a pitot tube which points directly into the airflow. Because the pitot tube and capsule have no exit, all the air entering the tube is brought to a complete stand still. This means all the air's kinetic energy is converted to pressure energy (static pressure) because the air's total energy must remain constant. Now the total pressure shows itself as an increase in static pressure

The capsule itself is surounded by static pressure. Consecuently, any expansion of the capsule must be enterely attributable to the increase in pressure inside the capsule caused by converting the dynamicpressure to extra static pressure. A mechanical or electrical system amplifiesthe slight expansion of the capsuleto display an accurate measure of speed.
It issometimes known as pitot pressure.
