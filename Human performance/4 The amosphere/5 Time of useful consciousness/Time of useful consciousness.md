Also named effective performance time
Defined as the period available to individuals after being deprived of oxygen, during which they are still aware of their enviroment and capable of controlling their actions.

Time of useful consciousness (TUC), deppends a lot if the individual is physically active. Then it is normal that the cabin crew has more TUC than piots.

TUC varies considerabily between individuals:
1. Individualshealth
2. Do they smoke?
3. Body weight
4. Speed of decompression, explosive decompression reduces TUC
5. Level of physical activity.
