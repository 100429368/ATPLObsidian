Facts:
1. Total lung capacity is 6L
2. Residual volumeis 1.4L, it's not breathed out
3. Tidal volume is 500ml during quiet breathing (volumeof air breathed out and in)
4. Tidal volume increases as the demand of oxygen increases,up to the vital capacity.
5. Vital capacity is the maxamount of air that the individual can breathe in and then forcibly expel.
6. Reserve volume = current tidal volume - vital volume
7. Inspiratory reserve volume = amount of air breathed out - amount of air that could be taken.
8. Expiratory reserve volume  = amount of breathed out - amount of air that could breath out.

This values are used for ill diagnositc. 

Rate of respiration = 12 breaths per minute. This increases during exercise
