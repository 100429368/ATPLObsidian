Small,spherical structures grouped into clusters called alveolar sacks.  The wall (membrane) is thin (0.5 microns) and is completely surronded by capilaires. This maxes air contact.

There are about 300million alveoli in an adult lung, a total area about $80m^2$.
Oxygen molecules disolve into liquid before diffusing across the wall to plasma. Once in the bloodplasma, it's carried in the red blood cells. While sporting this interchange takes 0.3s, so the blood remains fully oxygenated.
