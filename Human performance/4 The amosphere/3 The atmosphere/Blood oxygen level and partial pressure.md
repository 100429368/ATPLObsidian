Human body can compensatea slight drop of $O_2$ because hemoglobin is good at maintaining the oxygen saturation level of blood. Hence oxygen saturation levels do not fall at the same rateas the atmospheric partialpressure of oxygen.



Min partialpressure of $O_2$ in the lungs is about 55mm Hg, below (10k,12kft) theres a significant decrease in mental function, starting wit learning hability and difficulty performing new tasks.



THREAT
1- Reduced partial pressure of oxygen leading to deterioration of mental functions. 
No oxygen occurs at 40 000 ft


2- Insufficient partial pressure of oxygen at high altitude, resulting in failure to absorb oxygen into the blood, leading topilot incapacitation
This happens in extreme conditions such as massive decompression at max cruising altitude.
