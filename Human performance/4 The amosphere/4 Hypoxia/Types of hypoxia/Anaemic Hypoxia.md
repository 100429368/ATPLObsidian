
Caused byreduced ability of the blood to carry oxygen.
Haemoglobin is an iron rich protein which plays a key role in carrying oxygen in the blood. Anaemic hypoxia may be due to traumatic injury blooc loss,resulting in a reduced number of red cellsand consequently an insufficient amount of haemoglobin available to carry oxygen to the cells. Also from blood donation.

Haemoglobin has a greater affinityto carbon monoxide, forming carboxyhaemoglobin,than oxygen. As a result of this,carbon monoxide poisoning and even smoking may resultin hypoxia.
