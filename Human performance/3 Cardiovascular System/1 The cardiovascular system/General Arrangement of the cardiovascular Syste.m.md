
The purpose of the cardiovascular system is to carry oxygen and nutrients to the cells in the body and to remove and expel the waste producesand $CO_2$ produced by the cells. The primary transport system for both functionsis blood.

The mechanism by which oxygen is absorbed into the blood and $CO_2$ is expelled.

The cardiovascular system comprises a pump (he heart)and a series of interconected channels,the blood vessels,which carry blood to all parts of the body. Blood vessels are divided into categories:

- Arteries which are the main trunk routes for carrying oxygenated blood from the heart to the body tissues.
- Arterioles,the smaller branches of arteries, which are capable of contracting and enlarging to ontrol the supply of bloodthe organs.
- Capillaries which are the very smallest type of blood vessels and are responsible for the gas and nutrient exchanges between blood and cells.
- Veins which drain deoxygenated blood from the tissues and return it to the heart.

There are approximately twice as many veins as there are arteries,so the veins hold a lot of more blood. This severes as blood store which can be called when required.
