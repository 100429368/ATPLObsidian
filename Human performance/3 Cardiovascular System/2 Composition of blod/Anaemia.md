Haemoglobin is rich in iron so a deficiency in blood iron can result in insufficient oxygen supply to the cells. Anaemia caused by iron deficiency can result from any chronic loss of blood. Other common causes of iron deficiency are peptic ulcers andpregnancy.

Anaema can also be caused by blood loss. Blood pasma levels restore very quicky after blood lossbut it takes much longer to restore the level of oxygen carrying red blood cells. This is why a timelimit is impossed between blood donating and flying.

One form of chronic anemia is particular to Afro-Caribbean population. The cause is abnormal haemoglobin which distorts the shape of red blood cells which can then block capillaries. This results in reduced blood flow.
