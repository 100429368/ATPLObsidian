1. Red blood cells (RBC): carry oxygen and provide energy as they travel thoughout the body. Their effective lifespan is limitedto an average 120 days, by which time they need to be replaced. Blood appears reddue to the red blood cells comrising 40% of human blood.
2. White blood cells (leukocytes): Produced in the bone marrow, they travel though the blood stream, fighting infection. After their lifespan of 12-20 days, they are destroyed in the lymphatic system.
3. Paletes: these repair blood vesselsafter an injuri.

The body is constantly producing new cells in the bone marrow
