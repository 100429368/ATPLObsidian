As the blood passes through capilares in the lungs,molecules of oxygen enter the bloodplasma.

Plasmais not a very good carrier of oxygen. If plasma were the only means of carrying oxygen,the heart would have to pump80 liters per minute to supply enough oxygen to the tissues. In practice this is not the case because the red blood cells alow the blood to carry 70 times more oxygen that would otherwise be the case . As a result the normal resting output of the heart is about five litres per minute.

The reason why red blood cells areso effective at carrying oygen molecules is haemoglobin. One moleculeof haemoglobin is capable of carrying four molecules of $O_2$. Consecuently when $O_2$ molecules enter the plasma from the alveoli they are quickly picked up and absorved by the red blood cells.

When it is fully satured with oxygen, haemoglobin is bright red. When it loses its oxygen it turns darkpurple. This accounds for the difference in the colour of arterial blood compared with venous blood.
