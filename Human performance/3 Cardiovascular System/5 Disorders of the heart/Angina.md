During physical exercise the narrowed coronary arteries cannot supply sufficient oxygenand nutrients to the heartmuscle. Cardiac output failsto match demand so the victim becomes easily tired and short of breath when exercising.

The plaque build-up also prevents the coronary veins from carrying away all the waste products excreted by the cells of the heartmuscle.This results in intense chest pain during exercise (key simptome)

Angina is an acute condicion caused by a chronic problem. The most common treatment is nitrate based drugs taken during the attack.
