Is the word used to describe abnormally high blood pressure and normally defined as arterial blood pressure exceeding 140/90 in young adults.

Increased levels of arterial blood pressure require the heart to work harderto mantain the required stroke volume. As a result the heart muse thickens. Unfortunately this makes it more difficult for blood to diffuse into the muscle from the capillaries, leading to a reduction in the strength of the heart muscles.

Hypertension has a similar effect on the walls of the blood vessels.They also become irreversibly thicker and thus narrower. This increases the resistance to blood flow,requiring the heart to work harder still.

Hypertension is a major risk factor in severalpotentially fatal conditions including stroke,coronary heart disease and kidney failure.

Common causes of hypertension:
1. Age
2. Genetic Factors
3. Obesity

Also excessive alcohol consumption,smoking,stress,high salt intake,lack of exercise...

It can also be caused by an underlying condition like: pregnancy,endocrine disorders, kidney disease and brain damage.

Hypertension can disqualify from pilot's licence.
