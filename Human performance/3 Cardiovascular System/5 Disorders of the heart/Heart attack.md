The loss of blood supply to part of the muscle prevents it from contracting properly, then blood pressuredrops. Baroreceptos in the wall of the arteries detect pressure drop and trigger the sympathetic nervous systeminto action.  Adrenaline is released causing heart rate to increase.

These two actions give rise to a high pulse ratecombined with the palepallorof the skin which,along with severe chest pain, make up the classic symptoms of a heart atack.
