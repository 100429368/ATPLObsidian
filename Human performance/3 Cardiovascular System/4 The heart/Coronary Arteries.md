The heart muscle is large and very active. Consequently it requires a large supply of blood to feed its considerable requirement for nutrients and oxygen. This is the function of the coronary arteries. 

They wrap around the outside of the heart. The ventricles, because they do the vast majority of the work, are provided with a noticeably richer supplyof arteries than the atria.

Blockage of the coronary arteries can lead to insufficient supplies of blood to the heart muscle.
