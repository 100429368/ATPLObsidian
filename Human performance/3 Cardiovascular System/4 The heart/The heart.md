The heart's pumping action is created by strong contractions of the myocardium which surrounds the heaart. This contraction reduces the heart's internal volume and thus increases the pressure. Blood flows when the arterial pressure exceeds the presure in the veins.
After contracting, the muscle relaxes to allow the chambers to expand and take in more blood.
Blood is pumped from the heart thought the pulmonary system back into the heart. From there the highly oxygenated blood is pumped back out to various locations in the body.
