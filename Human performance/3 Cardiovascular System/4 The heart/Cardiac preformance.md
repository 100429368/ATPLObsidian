The activity and health of the heart can be measured in variety of ways. For flight crew this will include routine measurement of pulse, blood pressure, heart sounds and electrical activity. It is usefull therefore for us to understand these measurements are derived and what they signify. The heart muscle's repeating pattern of contration and relaxation is called the cardiac cycle. The cycle comprises two phases:

1. Diastole. The muscle is relaxed and all four chambers fill with blood
2. Systole. The muscle contracts,squeezing the chambers ,forcing blood to flow through the two circulation systems.

Blood pressure is lowst at the end of the diastole phase,when the chambers are completely relaxed and highest during systole.
