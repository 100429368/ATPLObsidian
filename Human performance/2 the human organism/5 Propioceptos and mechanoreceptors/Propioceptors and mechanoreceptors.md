* Propioceptors are found inside muscles. They detect and relay very precise information aboyt length of extension of each muscle.
* Mechanoreceptors are located inside tendons,close to their attachment points. They transmit very precise information about tension in a muscle.


This information is used to calculateposition and orientation of body parts. Tension information i also used to assesthe amount of weight being carried by a limb.

The greatest density of propioceptors is found in the areas with require finest control. Mechanoreceptors allow you to make fine adjustments to the backp pressure on the column when executing a tight turn.

The information from propioceptors is used in lower brain functions to coordinate movement of limbs. Also used at a relatively high level to control voluntary movement.
