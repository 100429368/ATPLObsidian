After a nerve signal has been initiated, a minimum resting timeis required untill the next signal can be generated. For a while afterwards(around 5us) the nerve requires a much larger stimulis to generate a signal. 

A repeated stimuli is one of the factors underlying sensory adaption. 
- Sensory adaption is a decrease in the physiological response as the result of repeated exposure to a stimulus.
Example: wristband, you feel it only at the beginning. Sensory adaption takes place at the sensory sites.  (Wrist in the example)

- Sensory habituation takesplacein the central nerous system. Weak sensory signals in the peripheres trigger progressively smaller responses in the brain. In somecircuits, stronger sensory signals may actiely inhibit weaker signals. The effet is similar but habituation lastslonger than sensory adaption. 

Habituation is a potential threat.  A repeated stimilus results in less brain signals.
