A solid state sensor uses a sealed evacuated champer. One end of the chamber is sealed by a flexible diaphagm made from a wter of silicon.
Changes in ambient pressure cause the diaphragm to distort slightly.
The levelof distortion is indirectly measured by piezoelectric strain gauges mounted on the diagphagm.

An alternative method of measuring the diaphagm's distortion is to measure the change in capacitance between two plates,one mountedon the diaphragm and the other fixed to the case.
