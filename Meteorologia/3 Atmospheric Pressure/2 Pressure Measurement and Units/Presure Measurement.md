- Mercury barometer
The apparatus commprises an evacuated tube partially filled with mercury Hg

Atmospheric pressure exerts a force on the mercury, driving it up the tube. If the atmospheric pressure drops,the weight of the mercury column overcomes the force of atmospheric pressure and the column of mercury will also drop. The height of the mercury column is directly proportional to the atmospheric pressure.

This mercury-based instrument is the reason why atmospheric pressure used to be inHg or mmHg

It is important that the tube is evacuated,which means that there is no air inside. If there was air inside the tube it would quickly become compressed as the mercury column rose thus opposing and eventually cancelling the efect of atmospheric pressure.
