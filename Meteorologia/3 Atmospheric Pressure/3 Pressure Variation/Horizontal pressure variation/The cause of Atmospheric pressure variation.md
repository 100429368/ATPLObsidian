
The formula of pressure is
$pressure = force/area$

If one area of the Earth has a higher o lower surface pressure than other,then it follows that there must be a greather or lesser force acting upon it.

Force is mass x acceleration. The atmosphere comprises trillions of moecules whih give it mass.
Earth's gravity gives this mass an acceleration of 1g
The Earth's gravity acting upon the mass of the atmosphere creates the pressure force.

The thing that changes over the surface of the earth,is the mass of the overlying air, not gravity.
The greater the mass of the overlying air,the greater the atmospheric pressure,and vice versa.
