There is an underlying semi diurnal variation in surface pressure:

- Highest pressure occurst at 10 and 20
- Lowest pressure occurs at 4 and 16

The size of dirusnal change varies with latitude,and is

1. Low altitudes:highest in the tropics, having a maximum variation of approximately 3hPa between highs and lows.This diurnal variation is significant and is usually the predominant cause of surface pressure changes throughout the day. In tropical regions, any departure from typical semi-diurnal variation can indicate that a storm is developing.
2. Mid-Latitudes: as in Europe,the maximum pressure variation is approximately 0.5 -1hPa. This variation is usually completely obscured by other local factors.
3. Polar regions: altmost non -existent
