Diagrams of variations in surface pressure are called surface pressure charts or synoptic charts.
Developed by Francis Galton in 1863.

The surface pressure at top of Everest is very low because it would give the presure value at 30 000ft

To obtain an accurate surface pressure chart, the readings must be with respect to a common datum,in other words wemust remove the height component of the pressure observation

The most common datum used is mean sea level so these presure charts are called mean sea level pressure charts.

Mean sea level pressure values are obtained by calculation. The mean sea level pressure of a reporting station corrected for elevation is called QFF. Each QFF value is plotted on a surface pressure chart and lines are drawn to join up places to equal pressure

Lines of equal pressure are ISOBARS. They are usually drawn at intervals of 4hPa. However regional variations may be shown at different intervals.

These patters can give us a lot of information about the likely weather conditions in each area.
