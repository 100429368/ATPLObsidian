 $P = \rho RT$
Since $\rho R$ is constant,then the temperture willincrease when we increase the pressure.

Subsidence inversions form as a result of air gradually sinking over a wide area. As the air descends,it is compressed by the increasing pressure in the lower atmosphere. This compression warmsthe air adiabatically (adiabatic warming). It generally warms at a faster ratethan the surrounding static air. However there is air immediately above the surface that has not descended from above and therefore has notwarmed this way. This airis cooler than the air immediately above it and,thus, an inversion forms.

Subsidence inversions are common over the northern continents in winter and over the subtropical oceans. These regions encounter subsiding air because they are located under global bands of high pressure.
