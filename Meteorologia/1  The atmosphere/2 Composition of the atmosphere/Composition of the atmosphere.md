The atmosphere is a mixture of gases, but it also includes other non gaseous substances like water , smoke, dust and ash. This mixture is air.

Up to a height about 60km the composition of gases remains very consistent, above this the gravity changes this. 
Ozone is an exception.

$CO_2$ is present in low proportions, but has an important effect on the global climate because it strongly absorbs long wave terrestrial radiation, then actin as a heat source in the atmosphere.

$O_3$ ozone also plays a significant role by absorbing short-wave solar radiation. It to acts as heat source in the atmosphere.

When $H_2 O$ condenses into a liquid as water, turns into solid form as ice,it gives rise to visible weather phenomena such as clouds and rain. Water vapour can comprise up to 4% of the total volume of the atmosphere.

Other particles such as dust,only affect to visibility and act as a nuclei around wich water vapour condenses to make water roplets or ice crystals.
