ICAO = Civil Aviation Organisation
Has defined a standard average atmosphere, which models the conditions from just below mean sea levelup to 32km. 

At mean sea level:
* Temperature = 15ºC
* Pressure = 1013.24 hPa
* Density = 1.225 kg/$m^3$

For the surace upwards

* -0.65ºC/100m up to 11km where temperature is -56.5ºC
* A constant temperature from 11km to 20km
* +0.1ºC/100m from 20 to 32km
